Managed Portal Vaults is the opposite choice, made deliberately: you hand the operational burden to us and we take on the obligations that come with it. Everything below describes how we carry them.

What is included

Four things you stop doing

A managed vault replaces the client software, the key handling, the second-factor juggling and the morning portal rounds.

Key custody in a dedicated vault

Your investor key is generated inside a hardware security module in an environment provisioned for you alone. It is non-exportable: it can sign, it cannot leave. Every signature is logged with the request it authorised. When you leave, the key material is destroyed and we hand you the destruction record.

Second factors, handled

Portals that still require one-time codes are linked with dedicated authenticator seeds held in the same vault. Where a portal only offers SMS, you get a dedicated number used for nothing else. Codes are consumed by the delivery pipeline and never shown to staff. Email-based codes are routed to a mailbox that exists only for your vault.

Document delivery, on your terms

New documents, capital call notices, distribution notices and statements are collected from every linked portal and delivered to your SFTP server, SharePoint or Google Drive, your document management system, a mailbox, or our API. Normalised to the ILPA templates, filed by entity, fund and document type, with a daily reconciliation report.

A named operations contact

A person on our side who knows your portfolio, re-links portals when a GP migrates to a new platform, chases missing statements, and answers within the business day. For portals that offer no programmatic access at all, that same team collects documents manually under a limited power of attorney you grant us.

Security and compliance

How we carry the obligations

Isolation per client

Each vault is its own environment with its own key hierarchy, its own network identity and its own logs. A fault or a breach in one vault cannot reach another.

Non-exportable keys

Signing keys and authenticator seeds are created in the hardware module and never exist in plaintext outside it. Our staff cannot read them, export them or copy them to another vault.

Read-only by design

Vault links are scoped to reading documents and statements. No vault can acknowledge a capital call, sign a document or change a bank instruction on your behalf.

Every action logged

Each login, signature, download and delivery is written to an append-only log you can export at any time or stream to your own SIEM. Staff access to a vault requires a ticket and is itself logged.

Built for regulated investors

EU data residency, contractual audit rights, incident notification and exit assistance are part of the standard agreement, written to fit the ICT third-party requirements that insurers, pension funds and banks have to meet under DORA.

Portal terms respected

We link portals through OIPP where it exists, through sanctioned APIs where offered, and through consented automation only where the portal's terms allow it. Where they do not, our team collects documents manually. We never circumvent a portal's protections.

Onboarding

Four steps to the first delivery

Agreement and verification

Service agreement, data processing terms and verification of the legal entities you want us to act for.

Portal inventory

We list every portal across your positions from the registry and your subscription documents, and agree a delivery schedule and destination.

Linking

Your vault is provisioned. On a call with you, each portal is linked to the vault key, or a limited power of attorney is set up for portals without programmatic access.

Delivery starts

Documents begin to arrive in your destination within the first cycle. You receive the first reconciliation report and your operations contact.

Questions we get

Plain answers

Do you see our documents?

Yes. In a managed vault documents pass through infrastructure we operate. They are encrypted at rest under your vault key, deleted after delivery unless you ask us to retain them, and staff cannot open a vault without a logged ticket. If that is not acceptable, the self-managed standard is the right choice and it is free.

Can we take the keys with us?

Investor keys are non-exportable by design, so no. On exit we generate a new key under your control, re-link your portals to it, and destroy the vault key with a signed destruction record. Nothing about your portal accounts changes.

Who is this for?

Family offices, fund-of-funds teams, insurers and pension funds, and fund administrators who want programmatic access to every portal without running software or managing keys. If you have an engineering team, start with the open standard.

What about portals that forbid automation?

We do not automate them. Our operations team collects documents manually under a limited power of attorney, on the same schedule, into the same delivery. The registry marks these portals so you know which ones they are.

Where is the vault hosted?

In the European Union. Other regions are available on request for clients with residency requirements elsewhere.
